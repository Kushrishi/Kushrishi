## Selected work

### [TrueMargin](https://github.com/Kushrishi/truemargin)

Medical-image-computing research on whether local variability from deformable image registration is informative about true spatial error. A controlled known-deformation study found positive anatomy-level associations across 10 held-out anatomies, while follow-up work documented blind spots and conservative held-out error bounds. Inverse-consistency error remains a strong comparator.

[Research overview](https://kushrishi.com/research/truemargin) · [Technical report](https://github.com/Kushrishi/truemargin/blob/main/docs/technical_report.md)

### [Model Regression Forensics](https://github.com/Kushrishi/model-regression-forensics)

A reproducible study of debugging regressions across versioned training changes. In two matched Banking77 benchmark worlds, simple visible-change baselines localized the planted change in both; the evaluated Grad-Dot and TracIn diagnostics each localized it in one. Current work asks when repair evidence is insufficient to support a unique historical explanation.

[Research overview](https://kushrishi.com/research/model-regression-forensics)

### [Autonomy Simulation Lab](https://github.com/Kushrishi/autonomy-simulation-lab)

A completed browser-based environment for path planning, dynamic replanning, noisy sensing, nonlinear localization, Kalman filtering, telemetry, and quantitative evaluation.

[Interactive demo](https://kushrishi.github.io/autonomy-simulation-lab/)
