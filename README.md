# Kush Rishi

**GNSS Analyst at Xona**

I graduated with distinction in Geomatics Engineering from the University of Calgary. My work at Xona focuses on GNSS and positioning data, while my independent projects range from biomedical imaging and model evaluation to simulation software for planning and localization.

## Selected projects

### Capsule Review

Local capsule-endoscopy viewer and fixed-budget frame-selection experiments. At 10% review workload, mean annotation-run recall was 49.35% for score-per-bin selection and 50.62% for uniform sampling across six exams with positive runs. Annotation runs are not expert-adjudicated lesions.

[Code](https://github.com/Kushrishi/capsule-review-public) · [Research summary](https://github.com/Kushrishi/capsule-review-public/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_COLLABORATION_BRIEF.md)

### TrueMargin

Image-registration experiments on 30 controlled cases across ten anatomies. Median anatomy-level correlation was 0.6841 for spread and 0.7203 for inverse-consistency error.

[Code](https://github.com/Kushrishi/truemargin) · [Technical report](https://github.com/Kushrishi/truemargin/blob/main/docs/technical_report.md)

### Model Regression Forensics

A local tool for comparing classifier predictions, checking release requirements and inspecting changed cases in an HTML report.

[Code](https://github.com/Kushrishi/model-regression-forensics) · [Example](https://github.com/Kushrishi/model-regression-forensics/blob/main/docs/deployment-comparison.md)

### Autonomy Simulation Lab

A planning and localization simulator, plus C++/Python tools for replaying recorded data and comparing model outputs.
