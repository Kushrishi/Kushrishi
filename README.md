# Kush Rishi

GNSS Analyst at Xona and Geomatics Engineering graduate working on reliable machine learning, sensing, localization, and estimation.

My professional foundation is in PNT/GNSS and measurement systems. My independent research focuses on evaluation, uncertainty, reproducible experiments, and claims that stay within the evidence.

## Featured work

### [TrueMargin](https://github.com/Kushrishi/truemargin)
My primary research project studies when medical image registration uncertainty is informative about local spatial error. Known-ground-truth and failure studies are complete; prospective calibration is active. Numerical calibration remains unestablished.

### [Model Regression Forensics](https://github.com/Kushrishi/model-regression-forensics)
Research on identifying responsible training changes through controlled counterfactual retraining. The matched-benchmark localization study is complete. Simple baselines localized both evaluated worlds; causal certification remains unestablished and continuation is under review.

### [Autonomy Simulation Lab](https://github.com/Kushrishi/autonomy-simulation-lab)
Completed v1.0 engineering environment combining planning, noisy sensing, nonlinear localization, Kalman filtering, telemetry, and quantitative evaluation.
