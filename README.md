## Featured work

### [TrueMargin](https://github.com/Kushrishi/truemargin)
Research on when local registration uncertainty is informative about true spatial error. The known-ground-truth study evaluated 30 cases across 10 held-out anatomies; follow-up work documented blind spots and conservative bounds on 30 separate evaluation anatomies. M4–M6 and retained-data diagnostics are complete; current exploratory development compares error-scale models. Useful clinical error bounds and superiority over inverse-consistency error remain unestablished.

[Research overview](https://kushrishi.com/research/truemargin)

[Technical report](https://github.com/Kushrishi/truemargin/blob/main/docs/technical_report.md)

### [Model Regression Forensics](https://github.com/Kushrishi/model-regression-forensics)
A reproducible case study of training-release debugging, with an experimental exact-label comparison utility. In the completed matched-benchmark study, simple baselines identified the planted root in both evaluated worlds. An external handwritten-digits fixture checks the utility against NumPy. Causal attribution remains unestablished; further certification work is under review.

[Research overview](https://kushrishi.com/research/model-regression-forensics)

Completed v1.0 environment combining path planning, noisy sensing, nonlinear localization, Kalman filtering, telemetry, and quantitative evaluation.
